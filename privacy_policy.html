Privacy Policy for DailyExpense

Last updated: August 20, 2026

DailyExpense is an offline expense tracking application. We respect your privacy and do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal information.

Data Storage

Expense information entered into DailyExpense is stored locally on your device for the purpose of displaying and managing your expenses.

Data Collection

DailyExpense does not collect personal information or transmit your expense data to any external server.

Data Sharing

DailyExpense does not share your expense information or personal data with third parties.

Internet Access

DailyExpense does not require an internet connection to perform its expense tracking functionality.

Third-Party Services

DailyExpense does not use third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or data-sharing services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be made available on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play Store listing.
